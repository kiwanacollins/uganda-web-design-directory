# Editorial Policy

This policy explains how the Uganda Web Design Directory selects, evaluates and presents companies. It exists so that readers can understand exactly how listings are produced and how the directory stays accountable.

## Scope

The directory covers companies serving the Ugandan market in:

- Website design
- Web development
- E-commerce development
- SEO
- Digital marketing
- Related digital services such as WordPress development and website maintenance

## How companies are selected

Companies are considered on the basis of publicly available information. We look at:

1. **Services offered** — what the company publicly says it does
2. **Portfolio evidence** — projects, case studies or live websites the company can publicly demonstrate
3. **Specialization** — whether the company has depth in a specific area such as e-commerce or SEO
4. **Location and market served** — whether the company actually serves Uganda
5. **Website quality** — the quality of the company's own website
6. **Public feedback** — customer feedback where it is publicly available and verifiable
7. **Relevance** — whether the company is a meaningful option for the audience reading a given guide

Inclusion in a guide is an editorial decision based on this evidence.

## Sources and verification

Listings are built from:

- The company's official website
- Other verifiable public sources (e.g. published portfolios, public business registries, public review platforms)

Claims that cannot be verified from public sources are not included. Where information is uncertain, this is stated rather than guessed.

## What listings include

Each listing records the company name, location, website, services, specialization and the date the listing was last reviewed. Where available, we note what the company is best for, using the public evidence available at the time of review.

## Rankings and "best" labels

Category guides are editorial recommendations. "Best" is a practical recommendation based on the evidence reviewed, not an official or certified ranking. Readers should treat them as a starting point and do their own checks.

## No payment for inclusion

There is no fee for inclusion in this directory. Payment never affects whether, where or how a company is listed. This rule is absolute.

## Corrections, updates and removal

Any company may request corrections, updates or removal of its listing at any time:

- Email: <hello@kicowebdesign.com>
- Open an issue on the [directory's GitHub repository](https://github.com/kiwanacollins/uganda-web-design-directory/issues)

Corrections are verified against public sources and applied as quickly as possible.

## Disclosure and conflicts of interest

The directory is published by **Kico Web Design**, a web design agency in Kampala, Uganda. Kico Web Design has its own listing in the directory. This relationship is disclosed openly on the [About page](/about/) and on Kico's [company profile](/companies/kico-web-design/).

Where any other relationship exists between the publisher and a listed company, it will be disclosed in that listing.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the directory grows. Substantive changes will be noted in the repository's commit history.
