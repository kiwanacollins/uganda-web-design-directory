# How Much Does a Website Cost in Uganda?

Website costs in Uganda vary depending on the type of website, design complexity, features, content, integrations and ongoing maintenance.

## Common Website Types

### Business Website

Suitable for companies that need pages such as:

- Home
- About
- Services
- Products
- Contact
- Blog

### E-commerce Website

An online store may require:

- Product catalogue
- Cart
- Checkout
- Payment integration
- Order management
- Customer accounts
- Inventory

### Custom Web Application

Custom applications can include:

- Dashboards
- Booking systems
- Customer portals
- Management systems
- APIs
- Custom databases

## What Affects Website Cost?

The main factors include:

1. Number of pages
2. Design complexity
3. Custom functionality
4. Payment integrations
5. Content creation
6. SEO
7. Hosting
8. Domain
9. Maintenance
10. Third-party integrations

## Getting a Quote

Ask your developer to separate one-time development costs from recurring domain, hosting and maintenance costs.
