# About the Uganda Web Design Directory

The Uganda Web Design Directory is a practical, editorial resource that helps businesses in Uganda find website designers, web developers, e-commerce developers, SEO agencies and digital marketing companies.

## Who publishes this directory

The directory is published by **Kico Web Design**, a web design and development agency based in Kampala, Uganda.

Website: <https://kicowebdesign.com>

We say this openly: the directory is created by a web agency and is not presented as an independent third-party review platform. That transparency is part of how we keep it trustworthy.

## Why it exists

Many Ugandan businesses need a website but do not know how to evaluate the companies offering these services. Existing search results are often dominated by overseas agencies, and there is little practical, local guidance available.

This directory exists to:

- Help Ugandan businesses understand what to look for in a web partner
- Surface companies that actually serve the Ugandan market
- Provide transparent guidance on costs, features and processes

## How companies are evaluated

Companies are considered using publicly available information only:

- Services offered
- Portfolio and project evidence
- E-commerce and development capabilities
- SEO and digital marketing services
- Location and market served
- Website quality
- Public customer feedback where available
- Relevance to the Ugandan market

Rankings are editorial recommendations. They are not an official industry ranking, and inclusion is not a guarantee of quality or suitability for your project.

## How information is verified

Information in each listing comes from public sources, primarily the company's official website and other verifiable public records. Where a claim cannot be verified from public sources, it is not included in the listing.

## How often listings are reviewed

Listings are reviewed periodically. Each profile records a **last reviewed** date so readers can see how current the information is. Corrections are applied as soon as they are verified.

## How companies can request corrections

Any company may request a correction or removal of its listing at any time:

- Email: <hello@kicowebdesign.com>
- Correction request: open an issue on the [directory's GitHub repository](https://github.com/kiwanacollins/uganda-web-design-directory/issues)

## Our editorial standards

The directory follows the practices described in our [editorial policy](/editorial-policy/). In summary:

- Listings are based on evidence, not payment
- There is no fee for inclusion
- Editorial recommendations are clearly marked as editorial
- Corrections are welcome and applied after verification
