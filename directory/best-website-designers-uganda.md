## How to Choose a Website Designer in Uganda

Before hiring an agency, consider:

1. Review their previous websites.
2. Ask whether the website will be custom-built or template-based.
3. Confirm who owns the domain and website.
4. Ask about hosting and renewal costs.
5. Check whether SEO is included.
6. Ask how the website will be maintained after launch.
7. Confirm mobile responsiveness.
8. Ask about security and backups.
9. Clarify payment and project milestones.
10. Make sure you receive access to your website and domain.
