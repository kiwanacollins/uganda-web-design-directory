## Compare the Full Cost

When comparing website prices, check whether the quotation includes:

- Domain name
- Hosting
- Website design
- Development
- SSL
- Business email
- SEO setup
- Content
- Maintenance
- Training
- Payment integration

A low initial price can become expensive if essential services are excluded.
