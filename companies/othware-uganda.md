# Othware Uganda

Othware Uganda is a full-service IT company headquartered in Ntinda, Kampala. In addition to website design, the company provides software development, cloud services, managed IT, web hosting, digital marketing and cybersecurity, positioning itself as a complete IT partner for businesses and organizations.

## Services

- **Website design** — responsive websites built on popular content management systems, with SEO built in
- **Software development** — custom enterprise software, web and mobile applications, management systems
- **E-commerce** — online stores with payments and integrations
- **Cloud services** — migration, management and optimization
- **Managed IT services** — ongoing IT support and maintenance
- **Digital marketing & SEO** — visibility and lead generation services
- **Web hosting & cybersecurity** — hosting and security services

## Best for

Businesses, NGOs and institutions that want one IT partner for their website and broader technology needs, rather than several separate vendors.

## Public portfolio

Othware publishes case studies on its website, including a learning management platform, a website and hosting project for a community-based organization, and a real estate web application.
