# Kipsi Web Design

Kipsi Web Design is a web design company based in Kampala, Uganda. The company builds professional business websites, e-commerce stores and promotional websites, and also offers web design training to individuals and businesses.

## Services

- **Business websites** — professional, mobile-friendly websites
- **E-commerce stores** — online shops with payment and delivery support
- **SEO services** — search engine optimization for local visibility
- **Web design training** — courses teaching web design and website management skills
- **Website management** — updates and ongoing maintenance

## Best for

Small businesses and startups that want an affordable professional website, and individuals or businesses wanting to learn web design themselves.
