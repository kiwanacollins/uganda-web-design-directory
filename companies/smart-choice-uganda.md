# Smart Choice Uganda

Smart Choice Uganda is a website design and development company based in Kampala, Uganda. The company specializes in custom web design and development, secure hosting and localized search optimization, and reports having served hundreds of clients.

## Services

- **Website design & development** — custom, SEO-ready websites
- **E-commerce** — online stores and marketplaces
- **SEO & local search** — localization for Ugandan search results and Google Business visibility
- **Mobile app development** — iOS and Android applications
- **Software solutions** — business software and web applications
- **Web hosting** — managed and secure hosting

## Best for

Small and growing businesses that want a website built with search visibility in mind from the start, plus hosting and app development support.
