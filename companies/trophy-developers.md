# Trophy Developers

Trophy Developers is a web design and digital marketing company based in Kampala, Uganda. The company describes itself as focused on websites and marketing that drive results, offering design and development alongside SEO, PPC and marketing services.

## Services

- **Website design & development** — responsive, custom websites for small businesses and enterprises
- **E-commerce websites** — online stores with shopping and payment functionality
- **Mobile app development** — iOS and Android applications
- **SEO** — search engine optimization for local and international visibility
- **Digital marketing** — social media, marketing automation and paid advertising
- **Website management & security** — ongoing maintenance and web security

## Best for

Businesses and organizations that want a single partner for both building a website and marketing it, rather than separating design from promotion.

Trophy Developers publishes a public portfolio of projects including work for an SME funding provider and an oil and gas company, alongside case studies on their website.
