# Cave Motions

Cave Motions is a digital agency based in Kampala, Uganda, offering web development, custom software, mobile apps, e-commerce and AI automation alongside motion graphics and animation. The company positions itself at the intersection of design, software and AI.

## Services

- **Web development** — websites and web platforms
- **Custom software development** — tailored business software
- **Mobile app development** — iOS and Android applications
- **E-commerce development** — online stores
- **AI automation** — automated systems for businesses
- **Motion graphics & animation** — multimedia and video content
- **Graphic design & branding** — identity and creative design

## Best for

Businesses and organizations that want software and website development combined with strong creative and multimedia capability, including animation and motion content.
