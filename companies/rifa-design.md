# Rifa Design

Rifa Design is a web design and digital agency based in Kampala, Uganda, serving clients across the country. The company offers website design, development, e-commerce, SEO, digital marketing and branding services, and describes itself as a full-service online growth partner for startups and small and medium businesses.

## Services

- **Website design & development** — custom, responsive and SEO-friendly websites
- **E-commerce development** — online stores with mobile money and card payments
- **SEO** — local and on-page search optimization
- **Digital marketing** — social media, content and PPC advertising
- **Branding & graphic design** — brand identity and creative design
- **Web hosting & maintenance** — hosting, security, updates and backups
- **App development & IT support** — mobile apps and technical support

## Best for

Startups, SMEs and established companies that want a website plus ongoing SEO, marketing and hosting handled by one team, at affordable price points.
