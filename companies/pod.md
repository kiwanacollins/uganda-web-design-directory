# POD

POD is a web design and marketing agency based in Bweyogerere, Kampala, Uganda. The agency describes itself as a brand solution for design and marketing, providing web design, e-commerce, SEO, social media marketing and creative design services, and reports over a decade of experience.

## Services

- **Web design & development** — creative UI/UX websites, delivered in as little as 30 days
- **E-commerce solutions** — online stores with multiple payment options
- **SEO** — search ranking, keyword and local search optimization
- **Social media marketing** — social and digital marketing campaigns
- **Google Business Profile** — local listing management
- **Logo & graphic design** — brand identity and creative design
- **Web & email hosting** — hosting and business email services

## Public portfolio

POD's published client list includes universities, corporates and organizations such as Makerere University, Roofings and a range of businesses and NGOs.

## Best for

Businesses and organizations that want a single team to handle design, development and marketing, from brand identity through to launch.
