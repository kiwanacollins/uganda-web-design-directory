# MTN Uganda

MTN Uganda is a mobile network operator headquartered in Kampala. Through its MTN Business division, the company provides digital and enterprise services that include business websites, e-commerce, cloud solutions, digital marketing and support for small and medium enterprises.

## Services

- **Business websites** — website design and hosting for businesses and organizations
- **E-commerce** — online selling tools and payment options such as MTN Mobile Money
- **Cloud solutions** — cloud hosting, collaboration and storage
- **Digital marketing** — digital advertising, social media and promotional services
- **SME support** — packages bundling connectivity, devices and business tools
- **Connectivity** — mobile, fiber and business internet services

## Best for

Small businesses, NGOs and enterprises that want a website as part of a broader package of connectivity, cloud and business services from an established national operator.

## Getting started

MTN Business solutions are engaged through business packages and the operator's SME and enterprise channels, with websites and other services ordered through the MTN Business portal.
