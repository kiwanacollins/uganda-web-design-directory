# Haclab Company Limited

Haclab Company Limited is a digital engineering studio and custom software development company based in Kireka, Kampala, Uganda. The company builds custom software, web systems and mobile applications, with a focus on enterprise platforms for Ugandan and East African businesses.

## Services

- **Custom software development** — ERP systems, business automation and transaction systems
- **Web development** — enterprise platforms and web systems
- **Mobile application development** — Android and mobile applications
- **E-commerce** — online stores and digital solutions
- **IT consulting and systems integration** — advisory and platform integration services
- **Database design and management** — structured data systems

## Public portfolio

Haclab publishes project work on its website and GitHub, including a corporate web platform for an engineering company, a digital auto workshop catalog, a fleet management and routing logistics platform, and an offline-first garage management ERP.

## Best for

Businesses and organizations that need custom-built software rather than template websites, especially those requiring ERP, automation and systems integration.
