## Company corrections

Companies listed in the directory may request corrections, updates or removal:

- Email <hello@kicowebdesign.com>, or
- Open an issue on the [directory's GitHub repository](https://github.com/kiwanacollins/uganda-web-design-directory/issues)

## Submitting a company

If you run or work for a company that should be considered, use the [Submit Your Agency](/submit-your-agency/) form. Submissions are reviewed against public information before listing.
